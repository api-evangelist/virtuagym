The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is rolling out a new “Click to Cancel” rule, which takes effect on July 14, 2025. It’s simple: if a member can sign up online, they should be able to cancel just as easily. This is your opportunity to modernize your cancellation process and make things easier for your members.
